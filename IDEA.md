Cyber Support Navigator that makes sure people don’t fall for scams or phishing attacks. It tracks your, SMS, emails and your everyday activities. It targets mostly elderly and people with non technical background. 

It gives each product a real job:

Gemma 4 or anyother model on JarvisLabs understands the request and explains information from a curated directory. The model runs on a rented GPU, not on the person’s device.

Temporal runs the follow-up process: schedule a check-in, retry if a notification fails, and flag unanswered cases for a human coordinator. That durable, time-based workflow is a meaningful use of Temporal.

Kepler is the operations tool for the organization running the service. Its team can use it to inspect the deployed app and investigate outages or operational problems.
